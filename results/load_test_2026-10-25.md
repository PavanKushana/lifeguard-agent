# Load test, 2026-10-25

Target: https://lifeguard-agent.com (Render free plan: 0.1 of a CPU core, 512 MB, one process, Frankfurt),
measured from Weimar on Sun 25 Oct 2026, around 17:00.

Each pretend visitor loads the page once, then asks /status every 5 s (like an open tab), with its own cookie (its own session). Each stage runs 60 s, then every visitor asks at the same moment (the burst). Only free endpoints, no AI runs. Times include the trip over the internet.

| Visitors | Requests | Failed | Typical (p50) | Slow (p95) | Slowest | Burst: typical | Burst: slowest |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | 160 | 0 | 66 ms | 116 ms | 147 ms | 114 ms | 143 ms |
| 25 | 353 | 0 | 60 ms | 78 ms | 381 ms | 318 ms | 325 ms |
| 50 | 690 | 0 | 64 ms | 116 ms | 442 ms | 645 ms | 662 ms |

## What it means
- **Nothing failed:** 1,288 requests in total (with the bursts), 0 errors.
- **Nobody would notice a slowdown:** with 10, 25 or 50 people on the page at once, a typical answer took
  about 60-65 ms and 95% took less than 120 ms, including the trip from Weimar to Frankfurt.
- **The bursts show the limit:** when everyone asks in the very same moment, the answers wait in line for
  the small CPU. 10 at once took about 0.1 s, 25 about 0.3 s, 50 about 0.65 s. That's about 13 ms per
  request, so the server can answer roughly 75 requests a second.
- **What that means for visitors (an estimate, not measured):** an open tab asks once every 5 seconds, so
  there is room for roughly 300 open tabs before the page gets slow. The limit of 200 sessions in memory
  comes first: after that, the oldest idle visitors get a fresh shop.
- **Not tested on purpose:** investigations. They depend on Token Factory and our spending limit (40 AI runs
  an hour for everyone together), not on this server's CPU.
