## Root cause

The sales-export cron job added 48 minutes ago runs a heavy COPY query every 5 minutes that exceeds PostgreSQL's statement_timeout (≈268 s), causing statement cancellations, API worker timeouts, and 502 errors from nginx.

Confidence: high. Evidence:
- API logs show repeated 'ERROR: canceling statement due to statement timeout' and 'CRITICAL WORKER TIMEOUT' every ~9 minutes (get_logs api).
- DB logs show the COPY query running for 268 seconds before being cancelled due to statement timeout (get_logs db).
- sales-export job was added 48 minutes ago and runs the exact COPY query every 5 minutes (recent_changes, list_jobs).
- Disk usage is normal and all containers are up, ruling out resource exhaustion (check_disk, list_containers).

## Fix

**Disable sales-export job**

1. `disable_job(job=sales-export)`

How to undo: Re-enable the job via the scheduler (e.g., cron) once the query is optimized or statement_timeout is increased.

What was done:
- turned off the job sales-export

Options that were offered:
- Disable sales-export job (recommended) (approved)
- Restart db and api, then disable sales-export job

## Follow-ups

- Risk of the chosen fix: Accounting team loses the every-5-minutes sales CSV until re-enabled
- Risk of the chosen fix: Current long-running query may continue for up to 5 minutes, causing 502s until it times out
- Long-term fix suggested by the investigator: Optimize the export query—add appropriate indexes on `orders.created_at`, `customers.id`, `order_items.order_id`, or export only recent data (e.g., last day) and/or increase `statement_timeout` temporarily while optimizing. Monitor query performance with `EXPLAIN ANALYZE`.
