## Root cause

The continuous data-export job deployed at 2026-10-26 12:48:37 writes large CSV files to /var/lib/postgresql/exports every minute, filling the PostgreSQL volume (50 GB) to 100 % and causing “No space left on device” errors that break order placement.

Confidence: high. Evidence:
- /var/lib/postgresql disk at 100 % usage (50 GB/50 GB) while root has 18.8 GB free (check_disk).
- /var/lib/postgresql/exports holds 31.6 GB of CSV export files (check_folder_sizes).
- Recent changes log shows continuous data export job deployed at 2026-10-26 12:48:37 (recent_changes).
- PostgreSQL logs show “No space left on device” errors starting 2026-10-26 13:38:37 (get_logs db).

## Fix

**Disable export job and move largest export file to root filesystem**

1. `disable_job(job=data-export)`
2. `move_files(from_path=/var/lib/postgresql/exports/export_2026-10-26_12:00.csv, to_mount=/)`

How to undo: Re-enable data-export job; move the file back from /export_2026-10-26_12:00.csv to /var/lib/postgresql/exports/

What was done:
- turned off the job data-export
- moved /var/lib/postgresql/exports/export_2026-10-26_12:00.csv (15.0 GB) to /moved-files/ on /

Options that were offered:
- Disable export job and move largest export file to root filesystem (recommended) (approved)
- Disable export job and move two recent export files to root filesystem
