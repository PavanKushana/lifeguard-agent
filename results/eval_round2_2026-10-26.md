20 faults (6 presets, 14 custom faults built by Ultra), each run once per setup. Every answer graded against the fault's hidden truth by the same grader (Super, fixed checklist: right part, right problem, right trigger). Every offered fix tried on a copy of the broken server. ## What round 2 says (written by hand after the run)

Round 2 ran the same 20 faults again with the two strongest setups from round 1, the same code and grader,
memory off. Nano alone wasn't repeated: round 1 already showed its fixes are weaker and it's 4-5 times slower.

1. **Both setups did better than in round 1, by the same amount:** the team 15 -> 17 right, Super alone 16 -> 18.
   Nothing changed in between, so this is run-to-run luck. It means a 1-fault gap between setups is noise.
2. **Both rounds together:** the team 32 of 40 right, Super alone 34 of 40. Neither got a cause wrong, and every
   recommended fix worked in all 80 runs.
3. **The difference that held up in both rounds is in the fix options, not the cause.** Ultra's options worked
   more often (52 of 59, 88%, against 55 of 67, 82%) and offered far fewer restarts nobody needed: 4 and 2 in
   the two rounds, against 9 and 12 for Super alone. The final call is what a human approves, so the demo keeps
   Ultra there.
4. **Fixed between the rounds:** in round 1 every setup blamed the one-off Redis crash ("Cache crashed and stayed
   down") on a 9-day-old upgrade. In round 2 both got it right. Same code, so this is also luck, but it shows the
   models can reason it out.
5. **Still partly in round 2:** the root disk full from debug logs (both setups), and for the team the two nginx
   faults (OOM kill without restart, bad nginx deploy), which Super alone got right this time. The checkpoint
   fault (logs say "corrupted data") came out right for the team and partly for Super; since 26 Oct the validator
   rejects new faults like it.
6. **Time:** Super alone was slower this round (36 s against 24 s), the team about the same (31 s). Token Factory's
   speed changes during the day, so times from one evening say little.
